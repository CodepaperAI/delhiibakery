## Design Context

### Users
Indian restaurant owners, caterers, cloud kitchen operators, and hotel/takeaway teams in Ontario who need dependable curry bases, ready curries, snacks, chutneys, and bulk supply quotes without wasting time.

### Brand Personality
Warm, premium, practical. The brand should feel like a serious commercial kitchen partner with restaurant-level taste, not a retail cafe or generic startup.

### Aesthetic Direction
Image-led Indian foodservice with editorial restaurant typography, warm cream and off-black surfaces, restrained gold accents, and real food photography. Avoid overly geometric sans-serif typography, oversized template copy, purple/blue gradients, generic SaaS layouts, and decorative effects that distract from the food.

### Design Principles
1. Let food imagery carry the emotion; keep copy short and specific.
2. Use human typography with a calm, readable rhythm.
3. Make restaurant-owner tasks obvious: view menu, understand bases, request quote.
4. Keep motion subtle and culinary rather than flashy.
5. Prioritize mobile readability, no horizontal scroll, and clear WhatsApp conversion paths.
